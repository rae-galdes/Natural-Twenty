Welcome to Wellis

[Spoken Intro]
Welcome. 
Welcome, come in, take a seat.
Bright strangers! Marina, get them some mead (laughs).
Welcome!


[Verse 1]
Welcome, bright strangers, come in from the rain,
Hang up your cloaks, ease your coin from your pain.
Warm by the fire, let your worries all spill,
There’s no safer bed than a mattress in Wellis.

We’ve lanterns that flicker, and wine sweet as sin,
And doors that stay locked from the outside, not in.
So, laugh, have a seat, let the evening commence,
We adore every guest who arrives with expense.


[Chorus 1]
Welcome to Wellis,
Where fortunes are made,
Where smiles are the sharpest,
And debts must be paid.

Here in Wellis,
Let your troubles all cease,
In Wellis we value,
Your comfort and peace.

Oh, dance in the lanternlight, 
Spin if you will,
You’ll wake a little lighter…
Welcome to Wellis!


[Verse 2]
Our streets hum a tune that the moon understands,
A rhythm of whispers and quick-moving hands.
Our watchmen are watchful, our Clerics devout,
And no one sees nothing when candles go out.

The harbour brings sailors with stories and gold,
They gamble, they boast, they grow careless and bold.
By morning the tide returns what it will,
And keeps what it fancies… 


[Chorus 2]
Welcome to Wellis,
Where shadows behave,
Where secrets are buried
And some find their grave.

Here in Wellis,
Tip well for the song,
We’ve ways of corrupting,
What lingers too long.

Oh, dance in the lanternlight, 
Spin if you dare,
Some lose more than silver…
Welcome to Wellis!


[Instrumental Break]


[Verse 3]
You’ve checked all your pockets? Your purse tied up tight?
Good habit to keep when you’re out late at night.
For coin has a way of deciding to roam,
And strangers forget when their silver went home.

Some fortunes rise quickly, some end with a spill,
Some wake with a headache… some never quite will.
If luck turns its tide, well, that’s part of the thrill,
Best sleep one-eye-opened… 


[Outro]
Welcome to Wellis!
